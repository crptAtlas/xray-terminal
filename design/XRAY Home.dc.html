HOW IT WORKS
01
SCANNER pulls every trade
every buy and sell of the token, straight off chain. no wallet connect, no signature.

02
LEDGER rebuilds each book
what each wallet paid, what it took out, what still sits there at current price.

03
TRACER follows the wallets
the same addresses across every other Pons token they touched. history, not vibes.
0x3f9a…c21e → $FEMUR $RIBCAGE $CALCIUM +16 more · 212 wallets with ≥ 5 past trades
04
AUDITOR grades the traders
average pnl per trade and winrate, wallet by wallet. says how many wallets each number rests on.
0x3f9a…c21e · avg +38.4% / trade · winrate 67% over 48 → SMART
05
SORTER bands the holders
holders grouped by winrate, five points wide, at most three groups - so you read "31% of supply sits with traders who win 60–65% of the time".
